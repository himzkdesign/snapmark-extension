SnapMark

Privacy Policy

This privacy policy describes how SnapMark handles information when you use the SnapMark browser extension.

Summary

  • SnapMark does not collect any user data.
  • All processing happens locally on your device.
  • No data is sent to external servers.
  • There is no analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • Screenshots stay on your device.

If you use features that save or export images (for example, downloading a file or copying to your clipboard), that action occurs on your device under your control. SnapMark does not receive copies of your screenshots.
